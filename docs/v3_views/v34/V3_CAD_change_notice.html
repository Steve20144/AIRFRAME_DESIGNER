The model was read from Fusion, weighed from the part-name labels only, and flown in the simulator. As drawn it flies (9/9 hover flights, no crashes), but its CG sits 50 mm ahead of the point where the nine fans share the hover load evenly, and the H-FLOW looks 20° off vertical in hover. Three changes fix that; everything else stays as drawn.

Changes at a glance

  1. C1
    Move the rear battery block 195 mm aft (Fusion +Z); lower its two outer packs 10 mm.
    Hover drift 1.86 → 0.53 m (median), pitch error 0.34° → 0.05°, busiest fan 55 → 50 %.
  2. C2
    Tilt the H-FLOW mount 20.4° so the lens points straight down while hovering.
    Position-hold drift on flow only 0.38 → 0.28 m (median), height noise 1.4 → 0.6 cm.
  3. C3
    Fix the weight labels: label 6 real parts, remove 9 unlabelled duplicates, reconcile the two jetfoils.
    Unlabelled parts weigh nothing in the model; the real aircraft is heavier than the 11.83 kg simulated.
  4. KEEP
    Jetfoil exit angles and nose fans as drawn.
    Measured on the B-rep: jet leaves at 69.0° / 59.3° / 49.4° (inner / middle / outer). A steeper inner foil (72°, 75°) flew no better or worse.

Axes used below

All positions are Fusion design coordinates in mm, as shown in the Inspect panel of the open design: X to the aircraft's right, Y up, Z aft (the nose is at negative Z). The simulator's frame is x forward, y right, z down with its origin on the centreline at Y = 450 mm; the notice converts everything for you.

C1 · Battery placement

The six 5200 mAh packs (760 g each, 4.56 kg together, 39 % of the labelled mass) set the CG. Only the rear block of four moves; the front pair stays where it is. A single 130 mm shift of all six packs balances the fans as well, but puts the front pair into the flight controller stack (V6X / RC08 parts), so it is not used.

  1. Select the four rear packs listed below (the ones at Z = +150 mm).
  2. Move them together +195 mm along Z.
  3. Move the two outer ones (X = ±85 mm) a further −10 mm along Y; at Y 435 they touch the All_mounts bodies near Z 285 to 342 mm.
  4. Rework the battery tray or straps for the new position; leave the front pair (Z = −150 mm) and the slide-in carrier unchanged.

"Middle" here means the point over which the fans share the hover load evenly. The geometric middle of the airframe (half-way between nose and tail, Z = +47 mm) is where the CG already is; the thrust of the tilted foils acts along the jets leaving the trailing edges, far aft and low, so the balance point sits about 50 mm further aft.

C2 · H-FLOW optical flow sensor

The aircraft hovers 20.4° nose-up. PX4 treats the flow sensor as looking straight down in its hover frame, so a sensor mounted flat to the structure sees tilted flow and a slant range (6 % long). Tilting the mount is the part of the placement that matters most in the simulation.

  1. Option A (recommended): keep the current spot under the avionics bay, lens centre at about (0, 388, 15) mm. Rotate the mount 20.4° about the X axis so the lens normal moves from −Y toward +Z (it looks down and slightly aft with the aircraft level on the bench). The view stays unobstructed; the lens is 82 mm from the new CG.
  2. Option B: under the nose, lens centre about (0, 373, -300) mm, same 20.4° tilt. Use it if the aircraft will hover above about 1.1 m: at the belly spot the inner foil jets (M5 / M6) land inside the sensor's view from that height, at the nose all jets stay outside it up to about 1.8 m. The nose ESC (ESC__M200g:2) sits directly above this spot; mount on fixed structure beside it, not on the slide-in carrier.
  3. Keep the lens at least 80 mm above the floor when parked (simulated: 386 mm at the belly, 482 mm at the nose) and keep the 42° cone below the lens clear of legs, straps and cables.
  4. If the hover pitch changes in a later revision, the tilt follows it: tilt = hover pitch.

Assumptions and limits

  • Fan thrust 36 N each, reaction torque 0.002 N·m/N, all spinning the same way (ATLAS_09B figures, not measured on V3).
  • The jet stays attached to the foil to its trailing edge; the thrust acts along the exit line there. Real jets separate early, which lowers the turning.
  • Only labelled masses are included (C3). Heavier unlabelled parts, especially All_mounts, will move the CG; rerun after relabelling.
  • The flow simulation has a perfect textured floor. It does not model dust or surface disturbance from the jets, lighting or lens vibration; the jet-in-view check covers dust only geometrically. H-FLOW field of view taken as 42°.
  • Legs are the simulator's provisional ones, parked at the hover pitch.
